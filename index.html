How it works

1. Choose Text to Video or Image to Video.

2. Enter your prompt or upload an image.

3. Select duration, ratio and style.

4. Click Generate Video.

The interface is ready for an AI video-generation API. The actual video generation requires connecting a video AI service to the Generate button.
